<h1 align="center">VergissMeinNicht</h1>

<p align="center">A self-hosted workspace for everyday responsibilities and repeatable work.</p>

## VMN in 10 points

1. See what’s due on **Today**, alongside **Weather** and the **Moon**.
2. Write reusable **Procedures** with Sections and Steps.
3. Follow a **Run** together and keep a record of who did what and when.
4. Set one-off or recurring **Reminders** and see them on the **Calendar**.
5. Share **Shopping** and **To-do Lists**.
6. Keep **Notes**, **Journal** entries and **Recipes** close at hand.
7. Organize **Documents**, **Contacts**, **Equipment** and **Maintenance**.
8. Explore shared **Places** on a **Map**, with photos and private visit history.
9. Make it yours with illustrated **themes** and a personal dashboard.
10. Host it on your own server and back up your **Workspaces**.
