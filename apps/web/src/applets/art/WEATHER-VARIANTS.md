Variant subject appended to the common prompt:

- Sun: a radiant warm golden sun with finely sculpted curling flame rays above the rock. Clear sunshine, no clouds, no rain, no snow, no lightning.
- Snow: a substantial cluster of pale silver-blue sculptural clouds gently shedding distinct crystalline snowflakes above the rock. No sun, no rain, no lightning.
- Rain: a substantial cluster of silver-blue rain clouds shedding clear luminous raindrops above the rock. Rain only: absolutely no sun, no golden disk, no sun rays, no snow, no lightning.
- Storm: a substantial cluster of dark blue-grey dramatic storm clouds with two clear branching gold-white lightning bolts and a few rain drops above the rock. No sun, no snow.

Generation option: `transparent_background: true`.

The card maps CLEAR to sun, DRIZZLE/RAIN/SHOWERS to rain, SNOW/SLEET to snow and THUNDER to storm.
Other/unknown conditions use the existing native condition symbol, avoiding invented sun or precipitation.
The normalized forecast supplies no day/night flag; the art represents the reported condition only.
Illustrations are decorative, never weather warnings. Current condition wins; daily outlook is labelled
and used only if current condition is absent, never to replace an explicit UNKNOWN.
